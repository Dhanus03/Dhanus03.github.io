Privacy policy

Mail Tracker is a personal app used by its owner to track job applications from one Gmail account.

The app requests the Gmail read-only permission. Google shares mailbox data with the app only after the owner consents. The app uses that data to detect application confirmations, interviews, assessments, recruiter messages, rejections, offers, and withdrawals.

The app stores the sender, subject, Gmail message ID, and extracted fields such as company, role, requisition, and status. It does not store the full email body. The sign-in token is stored encrypted. Mail Tracker does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or send it to an external AI service.

The owner can disconnect Gmail and delete the stored data from the app. Google account access can also be removed from the Google Account security page.

Contact: the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
