Mail Tracker

Mail Tracker is a personal job-application tracker for one Gmail account. It reads that mailbox with read-only access, finds application confirmations and later updates, and shows the resulting list to the mailbox owner.

It requests permission to view email so it can identify the company, role, and status. It keeps those extracted fields, the sender, the subject, and the Gmail message ID. It does not keep the full email body, send mail, or delete mail.
